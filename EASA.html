for motor engineering

Introduction

EASA is a global authority in the electric motor industry, trusted by engineers and manufacturers for its highly specialised tools and technical insights. The platform had been used for decades as a desktop based platform and used for motor rewinding design and multi motor comparisons and provide the motor library. The project focused on transforming this legacy system into modern, web based experience without disrupting the precision, speed and trust that long term users depended on. The goal was to elevate the product's usability and scalability and adaptability for the future.

Reimagining Legacy
Through Design

The Challenge

The existing application, though rich in data, felt cluttered and overwhelming. The learning curve was steep for new users, and specific workflows required extensive training. My challenge was to declutter the interface, introduce an intuitive hierarchy, and build a cohesive design system from scratch. All of this had to be done while maintaining the complex calculation tools and vast data tables that were integral to the product.

Design Approach

Our approach began with understanding the core users—engineers who view data logically, not just aesthetically. We mapped out their primary tasks, identified points of friction, and reorganized the information architecture. The design had to feel familiar but remarkably clearer, prioritizing data readability and efficient navigation.

Designing for Depth
Without Overwhelm

Grid System

We implemented a strict 8-point grid system to establish rhythm and discipline across the interface. This structured approach brought consistency to margins, paddings, and component sizing, which significantly reduced visual fatigue when scanning dense data tables and technical diagrams.

A Platform
Built for Growth

Custom Design System

To ensure consistency and speed up development, we created a comprehensive design system from the ground up tailored for enterprise needs. The system includes hundreds of reusable components, from complex table structures to specialized input fields, all meticulously documented.

Design
as Dialogue

Redefined Experience,
Continued Momentum

Close Collaboration

This project required constant communication with domain experts and developers. We established a regular feedback loop, presenting iterative prototypes to validate our assumptions. This collaborative environment ensured the design remained firmly grounded in technical reality while pushing the boundaries of usability.

The Result

The new platform successfully translates a complex, legacy tool into an accessible, web-based experience. Wait times for data retrieval drastically reduced, and the new visual hierarchy has been praised for significantly lowering the cognitive load on daily users.
